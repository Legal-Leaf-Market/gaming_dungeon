🧠 Harmonic Deep-Dive for Brayton

How guide tones, altered notes, and physics interact.

1. Power Chords vs. Jazz Shells Foundations

A power chord (1-5) has zero emotional bias—it's pure acoustic muscle. Jazz chords discard the neutral 5th and isolate the 3rd (major/minor identity) and 7th (harmonic tension). Dropping a single root or 5th by 1 or 2 frets instantly turns a heavy rock grip into a lush 7th or 9th chord.

2. The Law of 1-Fret Voice Leading Core Secret

In great chord progressions, chords don't jump; internal voices step. In a ii-V-I (Dm7 → G7 → Cmaj7), the 7th of Dm7 (C) drops 1 fret to become the 3rd of G7 (B), while the 3rd (F) stays static and becomes the 7th of G7.

3. Tritone Sub Dynamics ♭II7 Substitution

G7 and D♭7 share the exact same tritone interval (B & F). By swapping G7 for D♭7, your bass line walks chromatically down (D → D♭ → C) with zero loss of harmonic resolution.
