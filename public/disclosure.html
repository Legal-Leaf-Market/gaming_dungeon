How this site makes money.

Short version: some of the links here pay us a commission if you buy. It costs you nothing extra, we never handle your order, and we will tell you plainly which links those are.

WE ARE NOT A SHOP

Gaming Dungeon does not sell anything. We never take payment, never hold stock, and never see your card details or your address. Every product here belongs to an independent shop, and clicking through hands you to that shop's own checkout, on their own site, under their own terms. Returns, shipping, warranty and support are all theirs, not ours.

WHAT THE COMMISSION IS

When a link is a tracked one and you buy something, the shop pays us a small share of the sale. The price you pay is exactly the same as if you had typed their address in yourself. A commission comes out of the merchant's margin, not out of your total, and none of these merchants offers us a price we could mark up even if we wanted to.

NOT EVERY LINK PAYS US, AND WE WILL NOT PRETEND OTHERWISE

Roughly half the shops in this directory have a live affiliate arrangement with us. The rest are listed anyway, and link straight to the merchant with no tracking on them at all.

That is deliberate. The brief for this site was to build the right directory rather than the profitable one, so a shop that belongs here goes in whether or not there is a programme to join. We would rather point you at the right shop for nothing than at the wrong one for a cut.

Every outbound product link on this site carries rel="nofollow sponsored" regardless of whether that particular merchant pays us, because sorting the disclosure by our own convenience is how disclosure stops meaning anything.

WHAT THE MONEY DOES NOT BUY

  • It does not buy a position. The default order on every shelf is a shuffle that deliberately spreads the shops out. Nobody has paid to be at the top, and there is nowhere to pay for it.
  • It does not buy inclusion. A shop is listed because it stocks something that belongs in one of these rooms. We have turned down merchants paying far more than anyone here, and the reasons are written down in the code.
  • It does not buy a review. We do not publish ratings or scores, so there is nothing to tilt.

PRICES AND STOCK CAN BE WRONG

Prices and availability are read from each shop's own catalogue and can go stale between a refresh and your click. The merchant's own checkout is always the truth. If a price here disagrees with the price there, the price there is the real one, and we would rather you told us than assumed we knew.

THE ARCADE IS NOT SELLING YOU ANYTHING

The arcade has no affiliate links in it, no sign-up, no tracking of who plays what. It is there because a shop with an arcade in the corner is a better shop.

SISTER SITES

The links out to Kawaii Katz, Nicotia Market and Herbal Leaf Market go to sites run by the same people. They are not paid placements; they are the rest of the same project, and two of them are 21+.

ASK US

If anything here is unclear, or a link behaved differently from how this page describes, say so and we will fix the page or the link. A disclosure that is out of date is worse than none.
