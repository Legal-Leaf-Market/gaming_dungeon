Privacy.

We set no cookies and have no accounts. There is nothing to log into and nothing to unsubscribe from. We do count what the site itself is doing, and this page says exactly what that means rather than padding it out.

WE DO NOT COLLECT PERSONAL DATA

No sign-up, no newsletter, no contact form, no comments. We do not ask for your name, your email or your address, and there is nowhere on this site to give them to us. We hold no database of visitors.

The database this site does have holds merchants' product catalogues. There is not a single field in it about a person.

WE SET NO COOKIES AND SEND NOTHING TO ANYBODY ELSE

No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no Plausible, no heatmaps, no session recording, no A/B testing, no advertising network. Nothing about your visit is sent to another company, because nothing about your visit leaves this site.

WE DO COUNT WHAT THE SITE IS DOING

We keep a record of how the site is used: pages opened, things searched for, cards opened, things kept in a satchel, and clicks through to a shop. We read it to find out where this site is failing people, which is a thing we cannot see any other way. The searches that come back empty are the most useful part of it, because that is a list of what you came here for and we did not have.

To make that readable we have to be able to tell one visit's steps from another's, so each visit gets a random number kept in your browser's tab memory under gd_sid. It is thrown away the moment you close the tab, it is never sent anywhere but here, and it cannot follow you between visits or across devices. The record carries no cookie, no IP address, no name and nothing about your device, and it is deleted after 120 days.

That is the least we could keep and still answer the question. We are not trying to work out who you are; we are trying to work out which door people walk past.

THE ONE THING STORED IN YOUR BROWSER

Two things are saved in your own browser's local storage, and both stay there:

  • verda_satchel, the things you have kept. This is the most personal thing this site holds and it is held by you, not by us: it never leaves your browser and there is no copy here.
  • gd_arcade_invaders, your best score if you play Shelf Invaders in the arcade.

We never see either. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Three more live in your browser's tab memory, which is wiped the moment you close the tab:

  • gd_sid, the random visit number described above
  • verda_boot, so the opening title card plays once a visit rather than every time you move between pages
  • verda_paint, which of the painted background plates your browser found, so it does not have to check again on the next page

None of those leaves your device except gd_sid, which is sent here and nowhere else. Those five are the complete list, and a test in this repository fails the build if the code ever stores something this page does not name.

WHAT THIRD PARTIES SEE, WHICH IS THE HONEST PART

Loading any page necessarily tells a few other companies that a browser asked for something. We are not going to pretend otherwise:

  • Google Fonts. The typefaces load from fonts.googleapis.com, so Google sees your IP address and which page requested them. We use it because the sister sites do and a shared cache makes the pages faster; if that trade stops seeming worth it we will self-host the fonts instead.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps ordinary server logs (IP, page, timestamp) as any host does.
  • Neon hosts the product database. It holds merchant catalogues and nothing about you.

WHEN YOU CLICK THROUGH TO A SHOP

Product links carry an affiliate code so the merchant knows the visit came from here. Once you land on their site you are their visitor under their privacy policy, and most shops will set their own cookies to remember the referral. We receive a count of sales, not a list of who bought what: no names, no email addresses, no order contents.

How the affiliate side works is set out in full on its own page.

CHILDREN

This site is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anybody, so there is nothing we could hold about a child. Our sister site Kawaii Katz is the kid-friendly one; the other two are 21+.

YOUR RIGHTS

Rights to access, correct or delete personal data all need there to be some. There is none. If you believe we hold something about you, ask and we will look properly rather than send you this paragraph again.

IF THIS CHANGES

Adding analytics, a newsletter or accounts would all make this page wrong, so any of them lands together with the rewrite. A privacy policy that is out of date is not a technicality, it is a false statement, and we would rather be told than left to notice.
